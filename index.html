Research · Industry 4.0 · Machine Learning

Anomaly Detection in Industrial Process Data

Analysis and evaluation of machine-learning models for anomaly detection in industrial time-series data of walking beam furnaces. Team project with Laila Gruber (Vertiefung Berufsfeld – Tun/Forschen/Gründen). Isolation Forest is prioritized as the first practical model; One-Class SVM is the complementary comparison; the Autoencoder is a further, more demanding path.

  • Moving Average
  • PCA
  • One-Class SVM
  • Isolation Forest
  • Local Outlier Factor
  • Autoencoder

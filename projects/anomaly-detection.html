Anomaly Detection in Industrial Process Data

Systematic evaluation of machine-learning methods for anomaly detection in unlabeled industrial process data of a walking beam furnace (Stoßofen).

Project Overview

This research project compares several unsupervised machine-learning methods for detecting anomalies in industrial process data from a walking beam furnace. The work focuses on a systematic evaluation rather than on promoting a single algorithm.

Problem Statement

Walking beam furnaces are continuous industrial heating processes. Process variables are recorded over time, but labelled examples of faults or unusual operating states are typically scarce. Without reliable labels, conventional supervised classification is of limited use. The industrial task is therefore to identify unusual patterns in multivariate process data under realistic, largely unlabeled conditions.

Objective

The objective is to evaluate which machine-learning approaches are suitable as a starting point for practical anomaly detection on this furnace process. Suitability is assessed not only by detection behaviour, but also by robustness, interpretability, handling of unlabeled data, and effort required for parameterization.

Data

The study uses industrial process time series from the walking beam furnace. The recorded quantities include:

  • temperature-related process data
  • gas-related process data
  • air-related process data

The data are multivariate time series and are predominantly unlabeled. No assumed dataset size, sampling rate, or plant identifier is stated here.

Methodology

The same industrial process data are used to compare several unsupervised methods under a common evaluation framing. The comparison is systematic: each method is considered with regard to how it treats unlabeled data, unknown anomaly types, multivariate relationships, and practical constraints of industrial use. The aim is not to declare a universally best algorithm, but to identify which approaches are defensible for this process and this use case.

Models

  1. Moving Average

    A simple statistical baseline that highlights deviations from a smoothed local level in the time series.

  2. PCA

    Principal Component Analysis used to represent multivariate variation and to flag observations that are poorly explained by the dominant components.

  3. One-Class SVM

    A boundary-based one-class method that models a region of normal operation in feature space.

  4. Isolation Forest

    An ensemble method that isolates unusual observations through random partitions, without requiring labeled anomalies.

  5. Local Outlier Factor

    A density-based method that compares the local neighbourhood of an observation with that of its neighbours.

  6. Autoencoder

    A neural reconstruction model intended for more complex, possibly nonlinear relationships in the process data.

Evaluation Criteria

The methods were considered with respect to, among other aspects:

  • handling of unlabeled data
  • handling of unknown anomaly types
  • robustness
  • interpretability
  • multivariate processing
  • parameterization effort
  • extensibility

Results

There is no universally best method. Suitability depends on the process, the available data, and the intended industrial use.

Practical starting point

Isolation Forest

For the walking beam furnace process under study, Isolation Forest was prioritized as a suitable starting point for practical implementation.

Comparison model

One-Class SVM

The One-Class SVM is kept as a complementary comparison model alongside Isolation Forest.

Further approach

Autoencoder

The Autoencoder was assessed as a further approach for more complex and nonlinear relationships in the process data.

Conclusion

The results show that the most complex machine-learning model is not automatically the most suitable one for industrial use. Fitness depends in particular on:

  • the available process data
  • the requirements of the use case
  • the planned area of application
  • interpretability
  • robustness
  • parameterization effort
